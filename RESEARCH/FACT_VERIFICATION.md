## Original headline finding (before client confirmation)

No public source confirms a university called "Al Ahmadiya University", or any plan for Al Ahmadiya School to become a university.
The searches covered news, Dubai Culture, CAA/MOHESR context and Arabic-language queries.

Al Ahmadiya School is a **historic heritage site owned and operated by the Dubai Government (Dubai Culture & Arts Authority)**.
It is not an operating school. It closed in the early 1960s and was converted into a museum. Official and travel listings currently show it as **closed for renovation**.

**Status of the "university transition": UNVERIFIED — requires written client confirmation and evidence (licence application, MOHESR/CAA/KHDA correspondence, or an official announcement).**

## Disputed / unverified

- Founding year: Emirati author and former student Abdul Ghaffar Hussain argues ~1905. (Gulf News, via search summary)
- Closure year: 1962 vs 1963.

## Other observations

- The Collage 01 logo reads "AL AHMAIYA" (missing "D"). This is a typo to flag to the designer.
- Al Falah University, Dubai (a different institution) had its CAA licence revoked in 2022. It is unrelated, but worth knowing because both names surface in the same searches.
- Federal Decree-Law No. 31 of 2025 (in force from 1 Jan 2026) requires federal institutional licensing for new higher-education institutions in Dubai, including free zones. Any real "Al Ahmadiya University" would need this. (Secondary sources: uppersetup.com, academicjobs.com)
