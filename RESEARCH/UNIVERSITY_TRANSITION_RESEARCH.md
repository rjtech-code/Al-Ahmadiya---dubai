## Regulatory context (relevant to whatever the client provides)

- Federal Decree-Law No. 31 of 2025 took effect on 1 Jan 2026, with a one-year transition period.
- Under it, any new higher-education institution in Dubai, including those in free zones, needs a federal (MOHESR) institutional licence and programme accreditation.
- A genuine university launching "next academic year" (2027–28) would therefore normally have a licence application on file.
- Sources (secondary): uppersetup.com/en/blog/dubai-international-academic-city-in-2026-universities; academicjobs.com UAE higher-education law transition article.
